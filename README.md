# CHRONO-KILN // 88 (GEAR-LOOP)

A 2.5D Isometric Neon Clockwork Grid-Sliding & Route-Chaining Deckbuilder for the web.

## Core Mechanics
- **4x4 Isometric Clockwork Crucible Grid**: Slide rows/columns (`[⇄ SHIFT ROW/COL]`) and spin 2x2 quadrants (`[↻ ROTATE 2x2]`) to wire glowing Neon Conduit Tracks through elemental reagents (**Sulfur ⚡**, **Mercury 💧**, **Cinder 🔥**, **Salt 💎**) and hostile **Clockwork Automata 🤖**.
- **3 Signature Alchemical Route Recipes**:
  - **⚡ CALCINATION LINE (`1.8x`)**: Align 3+ nodes in a straight line or connect a Sulfur + Mercury + Cinder trio.
  - **🔥 SOLAR LOOP (`2.2x + 1E`)**: Connect a closed 2x2 circuit loop.
  - **💎 ROUTE // 88 CHAIN (`3.0x Overdrive`)**: Link 5 or more tiles in a continuous neon route.
- **Boiler Pressure & "Zone 88" (`60%–88%`)**: Maintaining Boiler Pressure between `60%` and `88%` grants a **1.5x Thermal Resonance bonus** to all Damage and Gold!
- **Night-Market Alchemist Stall**: Between sectors, draft new Gear-Loop cards and etch permanent floor runes (`2X NODE`, `OMNI-CONDUIT`, `CRYO-VENT`) onto your 4x4 grid.
